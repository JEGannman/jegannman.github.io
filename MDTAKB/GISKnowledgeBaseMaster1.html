Enterprise Access to ArcGIS Pro

This Section describes the procedure to connect to the MDTA Enterprise GIS through ArcGIS Pro. Please note that ArcGIS Pro must first be installed on your computer to continue with this guide—please contact the MDTA HelpDesk for assistance

  1. Open ArcGIS Pro
  2. Click “Settings” in the lower left-hand corner of the ArcGIS Pro splash screen
  3. Click the “Portals” menu on the left-hand side of the screen
  4. Click the “Add Portal” button below the list of available ArcGIS portals. (ArcGIS Online should be available)
  5. Enter the MDTA ArcGIS Enterprise Portal URL in the dialog box (https://gis.mdta.maryland.com/portal)

Follow the steps below to switch the active Portal.

  1. Open ArcGIS Pro
  2. With or without a project open, click the drop-down menu in the upper right-hand corner
  3. From the drop-down menu, you can get to the “Portals” screen by clicking “Manage Portals”, or you can switch your active portal by clicking the “Switch Active Portal” and choosing another portal.

To browse items in the Portal:

  1. Open a project in ArcGIS Pro
  2. Click the “View” tab along the top of the ribbon
  3. Under the “View” tab, click the “Catalog Pane” button
  4. In the “Catalog Pane” click “Portal” along the top of the window
  5. You can navigate and filter through organizational content by hiding folders, sorting, filtering, and searching using the search bar
  6. By clicking on the icons, you can view your content, content in groups of which are a member of, items throughout the portal, and items contained in ESRI’s Living Atlas (Living Atlas items are items that ESRI has shared with all users of ArcGIS—they contain various maps, layers, and applications that you may use within your projects)
  7. You may also search for portal items by name by using the search bar
  1. You can add a portal item to a map in your project by either dragging the item from the “Catalog Pane” to your map, or by right-clicking on the item and clicking and then clicking “Add to Current Map” or “Add to New” and choose a new map or scene.

Enterprise Architecture

This section displays the organization of the ArcGIS Server environments

This is a sandbox ArcGIS Server site where the users will be freely testing their applications and services. Typically, the development site runs on a small machine using an Esri Developer Network (EDN) license for ArcGIS Server. Once changes are validated on the development site, they will be applied to the staging site.

In dev deployment, the software components are installed on total of 2 machines including the Database Server.

Staging—This ArcGIS Server site is a clone of the production site. Testing at this level results in a decision to apply the changes on the production site or reject the changes and wait for a new iteration from the development site. The staging site is not used for development, just performance and functional testing.

Esri provides staging licenses for ArcGIS Server users at a lower cost than production licenses. In test deployment, the software components are installed on total of 4 machines including Database Server.

Production (PROD)

This is the site actually supports business workflows, the site accessed by real users. Absolutely no development or testing occurs on this site. Only changes that have passed the scrutiny of testing on the staging site are applied to the production site. In production the functionality and capacity of the base Enterprise deployment is extended with two additional servers

In production deployment, the software components are installed on total of 6 machines including Database Server. This requires 3 ArcGIS Enterprise 10.7.1 and licenses 1 SQL Server 2016 license.

As is is possible that our user base and activity increases, the components of our base ArcGIS Enterprise deployment may need to be scaled out with additional resources.
