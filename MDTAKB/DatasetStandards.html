MDTA has standardized on the ISO 19115/19139 metadata standard and the configuration and maintenance of ISO 19115/19139 metadata is required for all MDTA Enterprise GIS data, whether created internally or provided by contractors. Metadata may include information about how accurate and recent the data is, restrictions associated with using and sharing the data, and important processes in the dataset’s life cycle, among other characteristics. The MDTA Enterprise GIS Metadata Guidelines document should be consulted for specific metadata standards.
